About us

Hello, Hola, Hola!

Seastars Education Centre is the Spanish home of Sistars Educational Centre, a family school of languages founded in Poland in 2017 by three sisters — Daria, Marta and Mirela — all English and Spanish philologists with a lifelong love of languages, travel and music.

In L'Ampolla, on the Costa Daurada, that same spirit continues under Mirela, who moved from the classroom to the seafront to build a small, warm school where children and adults learn by speaking, playing, singing and — quite literally — making music.

We believe learning should feel like a conversation, not a test. Whether you're two years old discovering your first English words through a song, or an adult preparing your team for international clients, you'll find the same calm, encouraging, practical approach here.
